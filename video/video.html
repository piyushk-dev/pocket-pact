11 · AWS open source
Build It · Cedar

Who can do what?
Policy answers.

Owner records an expense. Supporter records a contribution. Both can propose a pact; the other accepts.

Cedar runs inside the API. Unknown actions are denied.

Wallet policy

Owner → add expense ✓
Supporter → top up ✓
Either → propose pact ✓
